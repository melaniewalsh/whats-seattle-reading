Introduction

This interactive explorer invites you to dive into nearly two decades of Seattle Public Library checkout data for authors included in the Norton Anthology of American Literature (1945-). The checkout covers 2005–2025 and lets you see which authors and works have circulated most heavily in Seattle during this period.

You can trace spikes of attention after major life events, like an author’s death, or explore how adaptations and cultural conversations bring older works back into circulation.

We encourage you to use the explorer to follow your own interests! However you approach it, the explorer is designed to give you a hands-on way to engage with the living, changing canon of American literature.
